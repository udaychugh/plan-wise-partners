Understanding Your Plan

What is NDIS Plan Management?

NDIS Plan Management is like having a personal bookkeeper for your NDIS funding. When plan management is included in your NDIS plan, it is funded by the NDIS and allows you to have a professional manage the financial aspects of your plan.

By choosing PlanWise, we handle the administrative burden of receiving invoices, claiming funds from the NDIS portal, and paying your providers. This means less paperwork for you and more time to focus on what matters most.

  • check_circle No out-of-pocket costs when included: When plan management is included in your NDIS plan, the fees are covered separately by the NDIS in your 'Improved Life Choices' budget.
  • check_circle Maximum flexibility: Access both registered and non-registered NDIS providers.
