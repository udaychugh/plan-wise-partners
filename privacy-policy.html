Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

PlanWise Community Partners is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website and services. We handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the site includes:

  • Personal information you provide (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial information (bank account details for plan management)
  • NDIS participant details and plan information
  • Sensitive health information, disability support needs, diagnoses, reports, or medical details where reasonably necessary to provide plan management services or where you have given consent
  • Website technical and usage data, including cookies and analytics information

Health and disability information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act. We collect and handle it carefully, only for legitimate purposes such as delivering our services, meeting NDIS requirements, or as otherwise permitted or required by law.

3. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect in the following ways:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • To process transactions and manage your account
  • To send administrative and marketing communications
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing Information with the NDIA and Providers

As an NDIS plan manager, we may need to share personal information with the National Disability Insurance Agency (NDIA), NDIS-registered and non-registered providers, and other authorised participants in the NDIS to:

  • Process and pay provider claims
  • Confirm funding, budgets, purchases, or supports
  • Obtain or provide plan management information required by the NDIA
  • Resolve payment, invoice, service, or participant concerns

We disclose only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We use consent, authorisation, contractual arrangements, or applicable legal requirements as the basis for each disclosure. We do not sell personal or health information.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Ask how we collect, use, disclose, or store your information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

We may need to retain certain financial, NDIS, and transaction records to meet legal, regulatory, audit, or tax obligations. We will explain if we are unable to provide access or delete requested information.

7. Website Analytics and Cookies

This website uses Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager measurement ID G-LGWRYDMW37. Google Analytics collects website usage information such as pages visited, approximate location derived from IP data, device and browser information, session activity, and referring pages.

Google processes this information using cookies and similar technologies to produce reports that help us understand website performance and improve our services. Google may process the data on servers outside Australia.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. You can also use Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

8. Privacy Complaints

If you are concerned that we have mishandled your personal information, please contact us using the details below. We will assess your complaint, acknowledge it, investigate it, and respond to you as soon as reasonably possible.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints, or by calling 1300 363 992.
